Rules

Walls are blueprints until a builder finishes them, and land only grows once they are built, so hire builders before you drag long lines. Laborers gather, guards hold the posts you place and shoot raiders in range, and raiders fight guards before they chew through walls. Put posts where the wall is thin. Build deep mines anywhere (normal terrain gives iron and stone, mountains give 2x iron and stone!) and farms on open ground, and use Demolish to tear down your own walls, posts, buildings, or resource nodes. Civilians move in when your people are fed, guarded and hold outposts, and they produce wood while they eat farm food. Iron from mines is needed for guards and raiders, and each guard drafts one civilian and each raider squad drafts two, so a happy, growing town is your army. The map is fogged. Send laborers into the unknown with the Gather tool (click bare ground) and capture sawmills and quarries for steady income and extra land. Destroy the other keep to win.
