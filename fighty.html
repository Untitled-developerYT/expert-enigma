Rules

Drag Claim Land over visible unclaimed tiles (1 food per tile); it does not need to connect to your kingdom. Unseen tiles, water, occupied tiles and other kingdoms are skipped. Resource deposits are fogged until seen and are depleted by hauling; storms erode exposed deposits once per storm. Mines produce stone and iron; a mountain Deep Mine doubles its iron output. Follow the optional milestones for one-time rewards; they never gate sandbox play. Close a wall ring to shelter enclosed civilians, buildings and land from storms; troops are not harmed by storms. Builders automatically repair damaged friendly structures; the Repair tool restores up to 25 HP per click: walls cost one matching material, while other structures cost one wood and one stone. Laborers automatically haul scarce resources from visible deposits; click a deposit to queue another haul. Send them to bare ground to scout. Recruit a commander before training raiders or sappers. Civilians staff captured outposts for extra income, produce wood, and support your army; food, safety and land sustain the population. Guards defend posts. Use the Technology tree below Build to research skills. Destroy every rival keep to win.
